Goals for Mac

Your triage, reading and tasks, on your own Mac.

Preview 0.1.0 · Apple silicon · macOS 26 Tahoe

Install the preview

Download Goals for Mac

Unzip the download, move Goals.app to Applications, then open it.

Fresh-device setup with a new user’s Google and AI accounts has not yet been verified without assistance.

Choose what you use

Choose Triage, Digest, Info Diet and Tasks in any combination. Your workspace is stored on your Mac.

Connect your accounts

Google is optional. To use Gmail, setup walks you through creating your own Google Cloud project and desktop OAuth client, importing its downloaded JSON and connecting your account. You can also reuse an existing desktop client. Organization policies may require your administrator.

Choose AI permissions

For AI features, connect your own ChatGPT account, check the available models and approve the specific use. Text tasks run only after you enable them and explicitly start a task; an independent review follows. They cannot purchase things, use websites or access files. Handoffs to other AI services currently use a saved context packet that you open or paste into that service yourself.

Keep your work through updates

Updates download automatically while Goals is running. Installation waits for a safe launch, and you can defer it. Active or unresolved work prevents activation. The updater checks the publisher signature, backs up the workspace and verifies startup. If startup fails, it restores the previous code while keeping current data.

Choose whether to send error reports

Error reporting is optional and starts off. If enabled, House Authentic LLC receives fixed technical error codes and coarse counters. Reports exclude message text, prompts, account identifiers, secrets and private file contents. Hosting providers may process IP addresses and network metadata. You can turn reporting off and request deletion from setup.
